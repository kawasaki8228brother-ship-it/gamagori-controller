# Pilot / selection / formal holdout separation

Document-only: no dataset assigned or opened. `DATA_PARTITIONS.OPEN.json` is
disabled, empty and non-executable; actual dates, IDs and hashes are null/empty.
No source data or approval original is included.

## Immutable roles before formal evaluation

1. Historical training fits model and normalization only from earlier available
   data. Each historical prediction's original source snapshot must pass its
   own point-in-time audit; summary cutoff metadata is not a substitute.
2. Selection selects w within the fixed A-family-only design. Neither B/C nor
   alternative features/calibrations become unreported confirmatory searches.
3. The first third-research operating day is pilot. It diagnoses capture and
   operations, never formal superiority, profit or promotion. Any reuse in
   later training/selection needs an explicit pre-holdout decision and record;
   pilot is never formal holdout.
4. A sealed future holdout evaluates the single frozen champion against its
   same-snapshot market baseline with the fixed M0 primary comparison. It does
   not choose the champion, w, threshold, exclusions or duration.

Training precedes selection, which precedes formal holdout. All actual cutoffs,
source/version membership and access records must be frozen privately and
independently checked before the holdout begins. The pilot's relationship to
development is explicit, not an inferred permission to reuse any observations.

## Registry design: reject leakage instead of renaming it

The future registry should reject:

- Any pilot/holdout overlap by **day or race**, even with a changed source label,
  model version, derived filename or later replay.
- Training/selection/holdout chronological inversion, duplicate race membership
  or an unrecorded permitted development overlap.
- A holdout race previously used for fitting, normalization, selection, feature
  exploration, threshold tuning, error-driven revisions or pilot decisions.
- Unknown original snapshot version, raw SHA, source revision history or label
  availability; selection/comparison also require pre-deadline odds SHA, with no
  current-table or final-odds fallback.
- Unsealed membership or changes after the freeze without explicit review and a
  newly designated untouched future holdout. Rehashed local metadata alone does
  not authenticate a new partition.

These are design constraints, not an actual-dataset registry validator or access
authorization. Static synthetic-metadata demonstrations are separate. The OPEN
JSON allocates no dates or zero substitutes. Raw/evidence references need exact
private originals, receipts and independent readback. Publish no private race
original, credential, secret URL or backup.

## Expanding walk-forward is not a sealed holdout

The existing expanding workflow can use earlier test dates in later training.
Those diagnostic folds are development evidence, not one untouched confirmatory
holdout. A retrospective replay or repeated old test cannot become future
holdout merely by renaming its partition. Existing synthetic workflow outputs
cannot establish real-data power, independent source availability or MODEL
superiority. No existing code/configuration is changed here.

## Seal sequence and stopping boundaries

The first pilot day alone cannot estimate sd_day with K-1: K<2 means undefined,
not zero. Additional pilot span is a separate review matter, not an automatic
extension or newly adopted minimum. After permitted pilot observations, review
their operational statistics and resolve the eight OPEN decisions. Freeze the
data definitions, source/PIT criteria, missingness/withdrawal rules, statistical
dependence/inference plan, minimum sample planning, single comparison, champion
and all artifact/partition hashes **before** holdout seal and first prediction.
The current pilot/holdout starts and seal times are undecided/null.

Predeclared completeness and exclusion rules apply without inspecting whether a
race helped the champion. No optional interim stopping, post-hoc extension,
weight/model switch or inferred pass-to-deploy permission is allowed. Safety or
invalid-data handling remains subject to the preserved external approval gates;
it does not authorize replay, repair, result rewriting or a statistical reset.
